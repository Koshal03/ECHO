Privacy Policy for Echo: Talk to Strangers

Effective Date: October 2026

Echo ("we", "our", or "us") provides a platform for spontaneous live audio conversations. We prioritize your privacy, safety, and transparency.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest Session Data: A temporary session identifier and device fingerprint are generated to enforce our 60-minute guest session limit and prevent abuse.
  • Account Information: If you create a permanent account, we store your chosen unique public username, email (optional/authentication only), and display name.
  • Sign in with Google: If you choose Google Sign-In, Google shares your verified email address and name with us. We store your email and a Google account identifier only to sign you in on any device. We never receive your Google password.
  • Device identifier (guest mode): To enforce the 60-minute guest allowance we store a random identifier generated by the app on your device. It is not your advertising ID or hardware ID.
  • Audio Transmission (Microphone): Echo requires microphone permission to transmit real-time audio during active stranger calls. We do not record, store, or monitor audio conversations on our servers. Real-time audio is streamed peer-to-peer using WebRTC.

2. How We Use Information

  • To match you randomly with other available users.
  • To enforce safety, blocking, and reporting mechanisms. Blocked users will never be rematched with you.
  • To track your coin wallet ledger and Google Play in-app purchases.

3. In-App Ads & Analytics

Echo integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display non-intrusive banner ads, rewarded ads for bonus coins, and natural-transition interstitial ads after calls. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

4. User-Generated Content (UGC) & Safety Policy

Zero-Tolerance Policy: Any harassment, hate speech, bullying, sexual harassment, explicit language, or scamming is strictly prohibited. Users can instantly block and report any peer during or after a call. Violators are permanently banned.

5. Data & Account Deletion (Google Play Requirement)

You have full right to delete your account and associated data. You can request deletion directly inside the app in Profile > Settings > Delete Account, or by emailing our privacy team at privacy@echo-strangers.app. All personal records and username reservations will be purged within 48 hours.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@echo-strangers.app.
